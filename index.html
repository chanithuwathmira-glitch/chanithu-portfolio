ABOUT • PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

A Commerce student with discipline, leadership and a digital direction.

I am a Commerce-stream student from Loyola College, with a strong academic record across both Advanced and Ordinary Level examinations. My time at school was shaped as much outside the classroom as inside it — through leadership, scouting, sport, media and debate.

As Senior Prefect, I learned responsibility, consistency and how to represent a school community. As a team member — on the field, in a troop and in a media unit — I learned preparation, communication and working for a shared result.

Now, I am building a career across digital, business and technology. This site brings my CV and portfolio together in one place: the record, the skills and the work that follows. My focus stays simple — Learn. Grow. Achieve.
